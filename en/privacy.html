Privacy Policy

Information about privacy and data protection in Math16 and on this website.
Last updated: November 2, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Math16 stores saved games, settings, and game statistics locally on your device. The app does not integrate third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics tools and does not transmit this game data to me.

This website consists of static HTML pages. When you visit it, the hosting provider GitHub processes technical connection data. If you email me, I process the information contained in your message to respond to your inquiry.

2. Local Data in the Math16 App

Saved games, settings, and game statistics are stored on your device so that you can play the game and resume your progress. I do not operate a server for this purpose. Math16 does not provide its own iCloud synchronization and does not create a user account with me. I cannot access your locally stored game data.

To the extent that this data constitutes personal data, it is processed to provide the game features pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. The data remains stored locally for as long as it is retained for use or until you delete it. Depending on the operating system, deleting the app alone may not remove all associated files; in particular, saved app data may remain on a Mac.

A device backup that you enable, such as an iCloud backup, may also include local app data. Such backups depend on your device and backup settings and are separate from synchronization by Math16. Backup copies are subject to the terms of the respective backup provider.

3. App Store, Purchases, and Apple Diagnostic Data

When you download the app or use the App Store, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, including information about your Apple Account, device, and downloads. Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy and its App Store Privacy Information.

In-App Purchase: Math16 offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full version. Payments are processed by Apple. I do not receive complete payment details, such as credit card numbers. The app verifies purchase status to unlock purchased features and restore previous purchases. No purchase data is transmitted to a developer-operated server. To the extent that I process personal data in this context, the legal basis is the performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Depending on your system settings, Apple may collect diagnostic and usage data and provide me with aggregated or non-personally identifying information, including crash reports. Math16 does not integrate any additional analytics tool for this purpose. Apple explains the relevant information and choices in App Analytics & Privacy. On iPadOS, you can manage sharing with app developers under “Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.”

4. Website Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub identifies the responsible entities as GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you access the website, GitHub receives the technical data required to deliver the page. GitHub expressly states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Depending on the request, additional technical information may include the time of access, the requested page, and browser information.

Using this hosting service serves the reliable delivery and security of this website. The legal basis is my legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The page cannot be delivered without transmitting the necessary connection data.

GitHub also processes data in the United States and other countries. GitHub describes safeguards including EU Standard Contractual Clauses and participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Details about recipients, transfer safeguards, and retention criteria can be found in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The GitHub Pages information used as the basis for this notice does not specify a particular retention period for visitors’ IP logs; I do not determine that period myself.

5. Cookies, Tracking, and External Content

The HTML code I provide does not set cookies, use local storage, or include visitor analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are provided by your device’s operating system, and images are loaded as part of the website. No external fonts, videos, maps, or social media plugins are embedded.

Ordinary links to other websites establish a connection to the destination only when you follow them. The respective provider’s privacy policy then applies.

6. Contact by Email

If you contact me by email, I process your email address, the content of your message, and any additional information or attachments you provide. This is done to handle your inquiry. The email link merely opens your email application; the website itself does not contain a contact form.

For inquiries relating to use of the app or a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other inquiries are handled on the basis of my legitimate interest in communication under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Messages are received and stored through my email provider WEB.DE (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH). More information is available in WEB.DE’s Privacy Policy. Transmitted data may also be processed by service providers involved in email delivery.

I delete inquiries once they have been handled and further retention is no longer necessary for follow-up questions, legal obligations, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. Where statutory retention obligations apply, continued storage is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR; where necessary for legal defense, it is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Contacting me is voluntary. Without a reply address and the information necessary to handle your inquiry, I may be unable to respond.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have the following rights:

  • Access to your personal data (Article 15 GDPR),
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Article 16 GDPR),
  • Erasure and restriction of processing (Articles 17 and 18 GDPR),
  • Data portability (Article 20 GDPR),
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Article 7(3) GDPR).

You can exercise these rights by contacting me at the email address above. For processing carried out by Apple or GitHub under their own responsibility, you can also exercise your rights directly with those providers.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement (Article 77 GDPR).

8. Right to Object

Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation in accordance with Article 21 GDPR.

I will then cease processing the personal data concerned unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or the processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

9. Automated Decision-Making

I do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy will be updated if features or data processing activities change, for example through the addition of online services or analytics tools. The current version will always be available on this page.
