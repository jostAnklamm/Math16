Getting Started

In Math16, you combine numbers from 1 to 16 using the mathematical clues on the board. Differences, ratios, sums, and products help you find the right numbers and rule out possibilities.

  1. Select a cell: Choose the cell you want to work on and enter a number using the number pad.
  2. Compare clues: Consider the mathematical relationships between neighboring cells and groups of cells.
  3. Make notes: Use Notes Mode when more than one number is possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the mathematical clues mean?

A difference describes the difference between two numbers, a ratio shows their relationship through division, and a product is the result of multiplying them. Sum clues apply to the group of cells marked by a bracket.

Pay attention to the indicated direction or order for differences and ratios. For example, a ratio of 3 is different from 1/3.

What is Notes Mode for?

Notes Mode lets you record possible numbers in a cell. This helps you keep track of several possibilities before committing to a number. Turn Notes Mode off when you want to enter a final value.

What can I do if I get stuck?

Look for cells with only a few possible values. Compare multiple clues involving the same cell, and check the numbers you have already entered. Notes can help you narrow down the possibilities systematically.

Can I zoom in on the board?

Math16 lets you zoom in on the game board. Use the zoom controls available in your version of the app to make numbers and clues easier to read.

Where is my game data stored?

Saved games, settings, and game statistics are stored locally on your device. Math16 currently does not offer its own iCloud synchronization. For more information, see the Privacy Policy.

What should I do if I encounter a technical issue?

Close Math16 and open the app again. Check whether a newer version is available. If the issue persists, send me an email describing what happens and how to reproduce the problem.

Please do not uninstall the app as a first troubleshooting step, as this may cause locally saved games to be lost.

Report a Bug

The following information will help me understand and investigate the issue:

  • Your device, such as an iPad or Mac, preferably including the model
  • Your iPadOS or macOS version
  • Your Math16 app version
  • The steps that lead to the issue
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • If helpful, a screenshot of the game board or error message

Before sending screenshots, please check them and remove any personal information that is not needed to describe the issue.
