How the Game Works

A 16 × 16 grid, numbers from 1 to 16, and mathematical clues: In Math16, you solve the puzzle by combining clues and drawing logical conclusions.

Differences, ratios, sums, and products reveal how the numbers are connected. Every relationship you work out brings you closer to the solution.

Solve It Step by Step

  1. Explore the clues: Examine the mathematical relationships between cells.
  2. Narrow down the possibilities: Use Notes mode to record possible numbers and combine the clues.
  3. Complete the board: Enter the correct numbers and solve the puzzle.
